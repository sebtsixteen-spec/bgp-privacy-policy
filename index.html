Privacy Policy

Brands Growth Partner (also operating as SellerScale) · Effective 6 October 2026

This policy explains what information Brands Growth Partner ("we", "us"), an Amazon growth agency also operating under the name SellerScale, collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers our website brandsgrowthpartner.com, our services, and the internal tools we use to manage our own LinkedIn presence.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us — for example your name, email address, company name and message when you contact us, book a call or become a client.
  • Client account information — when you hire us, the Amazon Seller Central / Amazon Ads data and reports you grant us access to, used only to deliver the agreed services.
  • LinkedIn data for our own accounts — our internal LinkedIn tool connects only to our founder's own LinkedIn profile and our own company page. It stores an access token and our member ID, publishes our posts and comments, and reads analytics (impressions, reactions, comments, reshares) for posts we published. It does not access other members' profiles, messages or connections.
  • Basic technical data — standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) kept by our hosting providers for security and reliability.

2. How we use information

  • To reply to enquiries and provide and improve our services.
  • To publish and schedule content on our own LinkedIn profile and page, and to measure which posting times and formats perform best.
  • To meet legal, accounting and security obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use LinkedIn data for advertising, profiling other members or any purpose other than managing our own content.

3. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us operate, under their own privacy terms — for example LinkedIn (publishing and analytics), GitHub (hosting and automation) and Anthropic (AI drafting of our own post text; no client or member personal data is sent for this). We may disclose information if required by law. We do not share client account data with third parties except as needed to perform the services you requested.

4. Storage and retention

LinkedIn access tokens are stored encrypted in our private automation environment and expire automatically (about 60 days). Post analytics are kept as aggregate numbers for our own reporting. Enquiry and client records are kept for as long as needed for the relationship and legal requirements, then deleted.

5. Security

We use access controls, encrypted secrets and the least access needed for each task. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing us. You can also revoke our tool's access to LinkedIn at any time in your LinkedIn settings (Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services).

7. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
